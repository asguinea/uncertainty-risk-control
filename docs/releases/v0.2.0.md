## Findings and limits retained

Strict GoEmotions budgets leave most comments for review. HumAID source-to-target transfer and target-local locked results have different roles and denominators, so their difference is not a paired causal recalibration result. The source full-pool milestone at 4,774 labels is one warm-up run. TweetEval retains the 2.5% final review-all outcome, sparse neutral selection at 5%, and 15.1884% observed selected error at a 15% budget.

Each budget uses delta 0.05 under the stated assumptions, without joint study/budget/class/event assurance or automatic protection under distribution shift. Human labels define the references. Final-calibration replay and aggregate regeneration do not reproduce historical training, inference, all warm-up prefixes, or source-to-assignment construction.

Included assets are original code, projected sufficient statistics, aggregate records, analytical figures and documentation. Source posts, row-level identities/labels/scores, keys, weights, tokenizers and product runtime remain excluded. [License scope](../../LICENSE_SCOPE.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) retain the HumAID humanitarian-research terms and unresolved acquisition-metadata limits. This release does not relicense upstream data.

## Candidate artifacts and verification

The planned package filenames are `uncertainty_risk_control-0.2.0-py3-none-any.whl` and `uncertainty_risk_control-0.2.0.tar.gz`. The wheel contains the method, CLI, all study replay/report modules and hash resources. The source archive additionally contains evidence, tests, examples, plotting scripts, figures and documentation. It supports the [complete reproduction guide](../reproduction.md).

The [installed verification script](../../scripts/verify_reproduction.py) builds both artifacts, inspects their contents, installs the wheel into a fresh environment outside the checkout, executes 68 unit tests and 150,000 synthetic trials, regenerates all three reports, and compares all figure numerical/provenance inputs exactly except environment metadata. Matplotlib stays optional. Publication-boundary and complete-history secret scans supplement manual review.

The local candidate receipt and SHA-256 inventory identify the actual reviewed build. Candidate citation metadata omits a release date and future tag URL intentionally; version 0.2.0 here does not mean a public release already exists.
